# Creative Brief — ig-ad

## Format
- Platform: Instagram Reels/Story
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Length: ~15 seconds
- Tone: Cinematic and reverent

## One-Sentence Premise
A custom-modeled African birthing chair — its backrest carved into two facing
elephant heads — is revealed as a piece of living cultural art, born from
digital design and CNC craftsmanship.

## Audience
Art & design / culture audience: people drawn to African cultural traditions
and craftsmanship as art, not primarily woodworking hobbyists.

## Goal / Call To Action
No hard sell. End on a "follow for more builds" beat — grow the audience for
future pieces.

## Visual World
- Warm, low, directional lighting that makes the wood grain and carved detail
  glow (think gallery or museum lighting, not a bright workshop).
- Quiet, deliberate camera moves — slow push-ins and reveals rather than fast
  cuts.
- The elephant heads are the emotional and visual anchor: symmetrical, facing
  each other, forming the chair back.
- Digital origin (SketchUp model, VCarve toolpaths) shown briefly as
  provenance/craft context, not as the focus — this is a story about the
  finished object, with the digital-to-physical journey as a supporting beat.

## Main Subject
The African birthing chair itself:
- Custom SketchUp model
- CNC toolpaths generated in VCarve
- Fabricated on a Shopbot CNC router
- Backrest formed by two elephant heads facing one another

## Suggested Shot Count
4–5 shots for a ~15-second cinematic reveal (to be finalized in
`03-shot-list/`):
1. Close, reverent detail shot of one elephant head (texture/grain).
2. Slow reveal pulling back to show both elephant heads forming the chair
   back.
3. Brief provenance beat: SketchUp model or VCarve toolpath dissolving/
   transitioning into the real carved wood.
4. Wide hero shot of the finished chair in context (gallery-like setting).
5. (Optional) Closing frame with subtle "follow for more" beat.

## Required References
To keep the chair and its details consistent across shots:
- Real photos of the finished chair (multiple angles, especially both
  elephant heads and the full silhouette)
- Close-up photos of wood grain/finish and carving detail
- SketchUp model screenshot(s)
- VCarve toolpath screenshot(s)
- Any Shopbot cutting footage/photos, if available

## Continuity Locks
- Same wood species, grain, and finish in every shot
- Same elephant-head carving detail and proportions
- Same chair silhouette and scale
- Consistent warm, reverent lighting style across all shots

## What Should Never Appear
- No fast, jokey, or meme-style cuts — this should not feel like a generic
  "satisfying process" video
- No unrelated branding or CTA overlays beyond a simple "follow" beat at the
  end

## Approval Question
Does this brief match the video you want, or is there anything to revise
before we move on to gathering references?
